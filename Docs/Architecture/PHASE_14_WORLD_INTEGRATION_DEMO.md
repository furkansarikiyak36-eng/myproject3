# Phase 14 — World Integration, Persistence Adapters and 119-Mechanic Demo Plan

## Purpose
Phase 14 is the integration-contract phase after Phase 13. It defines the safe route from independent subsystem foundations to a coherent, auditable demo. It must not imply that the earlier subsystem APIs are already integrated or runtime-verified.

The intended player experience and progression from first arrival through survival, resource gathering, log processing, physical construction, production, voxel terrain editing, farming, exploration and settlement growth are documented in [`Docs/GameDesign/MYPROJECT3_GAMEPLAY_VISION_AND_PLAYER_PROGRESSION_TR.md`](../GameDesign/MYPROJECT3_GAMEPLAY_VISION_AND_PLAYER_PROGRESSION_TR.md). That document is a design target, not evidence that those steps are currently playable.
